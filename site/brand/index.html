Brand guidelines

Chiswick Cheese Market

Read from the market's own website on 22 November 2026: the colours, fonts, logos, shapes and voice it actually uses. When an agent changes this demonstration, it keeps to this page. The same rules, for agents, are in brand.json.

Type

THE UK'S ONLY SPECIALIST CHEESE MARKET

Headings, menu, banner: Kapra Neue SB, upper case. A commercial font licensed to the market's site; this demonstration shows it in Barlow Condensed, an open stand-in, and never copies Kapra Neue.

The market is held every third Sunday of the month between 9.30 am and 3.00 pm.

Body text and buttons: Roboto Slab, 400 for text, 600 for buttons and emphasis. Open licence; self-hosted here.

Logos and shapes

Logos load from the market's own site and are never redrawn or recoloured.

Voice

  • Tagline: The UK's only market dedicated to cheese.
  • Headline: The UK's only specialist cheese market.
  • Banners are short, warm and in capitals: SEE YOU AT OUR CHRISTMAS MARKET ON THE 20TH OF DECEMBER!
  • Only the market's own words: an agent never invents a fact, a price or a stallholder.

How agents use this

Someone writes, from the site, can you make that more yellow? The agent does not pick a yellow. It uses Cheese yellow, #FAB600, with black text on it, checks the contrast, and says so in its answer. A colour, font or logo outside this page is not used without the owner's yes.
