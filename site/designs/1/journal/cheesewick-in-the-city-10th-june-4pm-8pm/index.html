Cheesewick in the City – 10th June 4pm – 8pm

5 June 2026

It is with great excitement here at Cheesewick HQ that we can share the news that we are taking Chiswick Cheese Market on the road for one night only to the launch of Lost Oasis. This immersive urban sanctuary, created by The Lost Gardens of Heligan, is set to transform the heart of Central London into a botanical escape and we can’t wait to be there for the opening night. Buttercup (our mascot Freisian) will be there too obvs… she didn’t want to miss it!

We’ve gathered a selection of our traders who will join us in bringing a taste of Cheesewick to the iconic grounds of St Martin-in-the-Fields on Wednesday 10th June between 4-8pm.

Join us and Steve Parker (our cheese guru) with The French Comté, Big Wheel Cheese, Gastromica Market, Brindisa, The Olive Bar, Drunk Cheese, European Fine Cheese, No. 2 Pound Street, Isle of Wight Tomatoes and So Gouda for some cheese in a lost oasis. Hopefully we’ll brie seeing you there!

Cheesewick in the City

Wednesday, 10th June 2026 4pm – 8pm

St Martins-in-the-Fields, Trafalgar Square

www.thelostoasis.co.uk @lostoasishq
