Free Monthly Cheese Market Tour

9 March 2026

Start your market morning off on the front foot. Steve Parker, cheese and wine author, speaker and judge is your guide to the Chiswick Cheese Market. He sets off from Cheese HQ at 9:45am and wends his way through the market, showcasing his monthly highlights, introducing the traders and meeting the market newcomers. Taste the wares, get a flavour of the market and when you’re done, please visit Steve’s own stall which overflows which the finest selection of cheese merchandise, as unique as the market itself.
