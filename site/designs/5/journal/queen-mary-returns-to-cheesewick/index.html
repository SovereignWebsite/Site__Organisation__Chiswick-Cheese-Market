Queen Mary returns to ‘Cheesewick’

Mary Quicke has been making Cheddar on their family farm in Devon for decades; she is a founder of The Academy of Cheese and has been at the forefront of the renaissance of the British Artisan cheese industry since the 70s. She is in a word, most definitely ‘The Queen’ of cheese making in the UK.

When Mary wanted to find out which age of their cheddar was the most loved, we were thrilled when she thought the best place to ask that question was here at ‘Cheesewick’. So, she is asking you to blind taste three ages of her cheddar with her and then you have the chance to buy your favourite from Cheeswick’s own King of cheddar – Roi. Don’t miss this rare opportunity to meet one of the most experienced, most informative and loveliest cheesemakers in the industry. Look out for Roi’s giant cheddar stack and you will find Mary next door waiting to say hello.
