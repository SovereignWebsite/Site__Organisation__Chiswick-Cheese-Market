Evening Pairing: 4 British Sakes with 4 Artisan Cheeses

For those of you who may not know Yuka, one of our team has two loves – cheese and Sake (her favourite drink from her native Japan). Brewed like beer, but enjoyed like wine what better friend could it have than cheese? We have teamed up with London Sake brewer Kanpai and together we are hosting a fantastic evening pairing 4 different British Sakes with 4 specially selected British Artisan cheeses.

This will be a tutored tasting so it will be a fascinating journey, experiencing some new and exciting flavours. Join us on March 13 at the Kanpai Brewery in London Bridge 6.30 – 8pm. Tickets are £35 – click here: https://kanpai.london/shop/best-of-the-british-isles-cheese-and-sake (link no longer works)
