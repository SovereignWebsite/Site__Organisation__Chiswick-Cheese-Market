World Cheese Awards

Its award season in the world of artisan cheese but this week heralds the pinnacle of all the competitions – ‘The World Cheese Awards’. It’s a British run event (@guildoffinefood) but has captured the world of cheese and now has over 5,000 entries from over 40 countries. It is the only truly global cheese award ceremony.

Our very own Fran Warde will be flying the ‘Cheesewick’ flag for us and joining over 200 esteemed International Judges gathering, with the cheeses, in Cordoba and on Thursday the Champion will be crowned.

Alongside this mind-boggling amount of cheese sits a newer but no less important competition to find the ‘Young Cheesemonger of the Year’. Run by @acedemyofcheese, we are the main sponsors of this competition with Peters Yard. The large field of entrants from all over the world has been reduced to 8 finalists showing off their skills and knowledge as fledgling cheese mongers. With several challenges in front of an audience and a quick fire round of cheese knowledge, the finalists will need nerves of steel while they show off their skills – may the best cheesemonger win!
