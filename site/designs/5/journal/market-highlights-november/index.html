Market Highlights: November

Despite the rain shower, it was a busy and very lovely market.

Madre mia!

As ever, our property master Lucy Lee-Tirrell, dressed Buttercup to beautifully reflect the surprising Spanish cheeses from Brindisa that we tasted on Cheesewick HQ. Rain or shine, nothing dampens Buttercup’s bright spirits.

Ooh La La!

We are so pleased that the Bonnie Wheel has taken up the role of bringing excellent Scottish cheeses to the market and that Ooh La La, a soft Saint-Félicien-style, multiple award-winner from Highland Fine Cheeses is back on form and at the market. You can expect a silky, unctuous, lactic cow’s milk cheese with some luscious added cream… ooh la la indeed.

We have a winner! 

Alfie of European Fine Food held a competition to guess the weight of the Tête de Moine cheese and win both the cheese and a girolle and we got a winner who guessed the weight on the nose. Congratulations!

You’re my favourite! 

Cheesewick visitors were lucky enough to meet Jane Quicke, third-generation cheese maker from Quicke’s Cheddar. She was conducting some market research so anyone who tasted their cheddars at three different ages will have had a say in which cheese they focus on bringing to market. Stay tuned!!!

Next market

Next market is 20th December and you’ll be able to taste the cheese voted best in the world at the World Cheese Awards last week as it is stocked by The French Comté and it is a comté so please come and taste the best of the best.
