Visit us

Plan your visit to the UK’s only dedicated cheese market

The market is held every third Sunday of the month between 9.30 am and 3.00 pm at The Old Market Place, Chiswick High Road (opposite M&S), W4 2DR.

What3Words: ///cherry.grabs.tubes

A unique destination for cheese lovers. Held every third Sunday of the month, the market celebrates the finest artisan cheeses from across the UK and beyond. Come and explore a delicious array of flavours in the charming surroundings of The Old Market Place in Chiswick.
Turnham Green station (District Line) is a 5-minute walk from the market. Chiswick Park and Gunnersbury stations are also nearby.
Use the Chiswick Police Station stop for routes 237, 267, 110, E3, and H91. For the Turnham Green Station stop, use routes 94 and 272. For the Turnham Green Church stop, use route 440.
Gunnersbury station is on the Mildmay line (Overground) a 15-minute walk along Chiswick High Road or connect to a bus or the District line.
Chiswick station (South Western Railways) is 25 minutes on foot or take the E3 or 272 bus.
Old Market Place is located next to Cycleway 9 on Chiswick High Road. Check the TfL website for updates.
Car parking is on-street and is mostly free of charge on Sundays but check first via signage on the street or PaybyPhone App. Free parking is available on side roads, with larger options at Sainsbury’s (Essex Place, W4 5UT) and St. Mary’s RC Primary School (book via JustPark).

Accessibility

The market is held entirely on a single-level outdoor space at The Old Market Place with no stairs or lifts to navigate.
Parking
There is dedicated disabled parking on Devonshire Road. For blue badge holders, there are many more options.

Assistance
Our friendly staff and volunteers are always happy to provide assistance or answer any questions during your visit.
