Nut Farms

Nut Farms are Kent-based producers of premium British nuts and oils, known for roasted cobnuts, English walnuts, nut butters and cold-pressed oils.

Committed to provenance and sustainability, they deliver authentic, flavourful nuts and nut products to markets, online customers, and food businesses, showcasing the best of British-grown produce.
