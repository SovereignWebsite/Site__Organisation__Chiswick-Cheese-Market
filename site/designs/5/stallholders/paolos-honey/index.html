Paolo’s Honey

Raw, unprocessed honey sourced from sustainably managed hives in Portugal. A great accompaniment for cheeses, particularly soft and tangy varieties like goat cheese or Brie. The range includes rosemary, wild lavender, oak, heather and multi floral honeys.
