Curd & Cure

Curd & Cure are the South East’s leading independent artisan food and drink distributor, supplying some of the UK’s finest speciality producers since 1988. Based in Kent, they work closely with a trusted network of small and large producers to source and deliver outstanding quality products.

With over 1,400 artisan deli, farm shop and foodservice lines available for next-day national delivery, and a flexible pallet consolidation service for Europe, Curd & Cure are a key link between great producers and great food businesses.

They are proud to be the exclusive UK stockist of the newly crowned 2025 World Champion Cheese, Le Gruyère AOP, and are delighted to showcase this exceptional cheese at the market.
