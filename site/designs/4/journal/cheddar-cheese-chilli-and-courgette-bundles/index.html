Cheddar Cheese, Chilli and Courgette bundles

by Fran Warde

Elevate the simple courgette to these delicious vegetable addition to any meal, you can easily change the cheese to your favourite but I like making them with a classic West Country Cheddar.

makes 6

preparation 25 minutes

cooking 45 minutes

4 / 5 medium courgettes

150g west country cheddar, grated

chilli flakes

freshly ground black pepper

Heat the oven to 200C

Using a vegetable peeler, draw ribbons off the length of the courgettes.

Arrange a layer, slightly overlapping the edges and ends of the courgette ribbons, making a sheet of approx 12cm high 48cm length.

Sprinkle with 1 6th of the grated cheese, chilli flakes and pepper.

Carefully fold and roll the courgette sheet from one end to the other and place end up in a silicone muffin tray or a lightly oiled muffin tin and gently push the top down with the palm of your hand. This helps make it more compact.

Repeat until all 6 are prepared top with a little extra cheese and place in the oven to cook.

Reduce the temperature after 25 minutes to 180C they should be golden and bubbling. Cook for a further 20 mins.

Serve and enjoy on their own or as part of a meal.
