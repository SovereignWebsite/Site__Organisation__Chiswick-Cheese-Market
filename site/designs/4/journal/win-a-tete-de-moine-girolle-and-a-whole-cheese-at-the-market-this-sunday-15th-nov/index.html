News & Cheeses & Spotlight & Lucy's Cheese of the Month

Win a Tête de Moine girolle and a whole cheese at the market THIS SUNDAY – 15TH NOV!

9 November 2026

Lucy’s Cheese of the Month is Tête de Moine made by the only family owned maker in the region, Spielhofer, and to celebrate Alfie @europeanfinecheese is offering the chance to win a whole wheel of this fabulous Swiss Alpine cheese, plus the crafty little gadget that turns it into beautiful finely shredded flowery rossettes (ready decorate any cheese board, buffet or just enjoy with friends). The ‘girolle’ works by turning a sharp blade in a circular motion allowing it to shred a fine top layer of the round cheese curling it as it goes.

The competition is simple – guess the exact weight of the cheese on Alfie’s stall and you will win the cheese and Girolle. Find Alfie and the cheese @europeanfinecheese this Sunday at the market.
