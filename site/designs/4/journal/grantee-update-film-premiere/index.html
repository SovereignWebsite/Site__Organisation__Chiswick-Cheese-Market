Grantee Update: Film Premiere!

30 October 2026

We said it was coming, and now it’s here: premiere!

As we’ve happily reported previously, our 2025 Chiswick Cheese Market Grantee, Jake Goldstein, has thrown himself into cheesemaking with an inspiring – and award-winning – passion. When Sam Brice, a marketing professional who tells the stories of independent makers, ran across Jake, it was a natural fit. RAW: The Story of a Small Cheese follows Jake’s journey from running cocktail bars in Brighton to making his own cheese on the Welbeck Estate.

The Chiswick Cheese market was pleased to also help sponsor the making of this films and it includes cameos from Chiswick Cheese Market members Lucy Cufflin and Fran Warde, as well as Tracey Colley of the Academy of Cheese, our co-sponsors of the grant programme.

We are organizing a viewing of the film in early 2027 so please stay tuned. 

RAW: The Story of a Small Cheese will be released online on Wednesday 4 November. See the trailer below.
