Event alert: A ‘gin’eous pairing of Sipsmith Gin and Cheese, 16th September

24 August 2026

We’re teaming up with our local gin distillery, Sipsmith, to offer an extraordinary evening pairing the very best British Artisan Gin by Sipsmith, with the very best of British Artisan Cheese from Chiswick Cheese Market. Kick off with a welcome G&T paired with handmade warm cheese straws, followed by 5 gins and 6 cheeses all carefully paired by Cheesewick’s Lucy, Donna and Lucinda and the Sipsmith team.
This is an evening not to be missed! 16th September 6.30pm at Sipsmith Distillery on Cranbrook Road in Chiswick. Tickets are available – link below:
https://sipsmith.com/distillery-tours/#id=smithery-sessions
