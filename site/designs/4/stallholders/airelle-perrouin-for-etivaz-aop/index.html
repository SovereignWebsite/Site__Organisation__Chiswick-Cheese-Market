Cheese producers and mongers

Airelle Perrouin for Etivaz AOP

Etivaz is made only during the summer months in remote mountain chalets, every wheel of L’Etivaz AOP is crafted over traditional wood-fired copper vats using milk from cows grazing on wildflower-filled Alpine meadows. The cheese is beautifully complex with fruity, floral notes along with a delicate hint of smoke.
