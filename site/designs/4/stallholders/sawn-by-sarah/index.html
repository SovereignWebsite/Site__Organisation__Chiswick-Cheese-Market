Cheese Accessories

Sawn by Sarah

Using live edge timber and occasionally blending in epoxy resin, Sarah crafts unique home decor and gifting pieces that highlight the wood’s natural grain, knots, and organic voids. Ready to ship offerings span from charcuterie boards, coaster sets, and egg holders to candle holders and sculptural side and coffee tables, each infused with warmth and artisan flair. For those seeking something personal, bespoke commissions are warmly welcomed to bring your vision to life.
