Easter Sunday at Cheesewick – 20th April | 9.30am–3pm

Spend your Easter Sunday grazing through over 200 artisan cheeses at the UK’s only dedicated cheese market. Alongside our legendary toasties, galettes, and arancini, we’ve got Easter treats galore.

16 April 2025

Spend your Easter Sunday grazing through over 200 artisan cheeses at the UK’s only dedicated cheese market. Alongside our legendary toasties, galettes, and arancini, we’ve got Easter treats galore: burrata focaccia (@gastronomicamarket), smoked trout tarts (@berkstrout – bonnet wearers get a discount!), tiramisu Basque cheesecake (@lamaritxu), and special babka bakes (@dalybread). Kids can join in the fun too – help Buttercup the cow find her five hidden friends across the market and win an Easter egg!
