Cheesemakers Grant

Cheesemakers Grant

At Chiswick Cheese Market, we’re passionate about supporting the next generation of cheesemakers. That’s why we’ve created the Chiswick Cheese Market Cheesemakers Grant – a special initiative designed to help new talent break into the world of cheese-making and take their craft to the next level.

The Chiswick Cheese Market Cheesemakers Grant is our way of giving emerging cheesemakers the tools, knowledge, and experience they need to succeed. In partnership with the Academy of Cheese, we offer this annual grant to deserving cheesemakers each year. Through the grant, recipients will gain Academy of Cheese certifications, hands-on cheesemaking experience, and invaluable support to enhance their skills and grow their careers in the cheese industry.

For a full 12 months, each grant winner will benefit from bespoke training and guidance, helping them to sharpen their craft and improve their business acumen. Additionally, we’ll offer them the chance to showcase their creations at Chiswick Cheese Market and connect with Academy of Cheese members around the world – providing opportunities for exposure, promotion, and growth in the wider cheese community.

If you’re a new cheesemaker looking to advance your career and make your mark in the cheese world, the Chiswick Cheese Market Cheesemakers Grant could be the perfect stepping stone.

Aims

  • Encourage and support new cheesemakers
  • Support new cheese development
  • Provide access to cheese courses and certification

2025 Awardees

We’re delighted to announce 2025 year’s two recipients of the Cheesemaker’s Grant. Both have impressed the organisers with their ethos, passion, and vision for the future. Their dedication to excellence and innovation makes them truly deserving of this recognition, and we are excited to support their journeys.

2024 Awardees

There were so many deserving candidates that we added an extra grant in 2024. The five winners, who beat off the competition from a host of entries and impressed the organisers during their interviews are Emily Tydeman from Suffolk, Finn Dunlop from Kent, Jo Shelton from Norfolk, Matt Gue from Sussex and Matthew Lloyd from Shropshire.

2023 Awardees

Our very first grants were awarded to Aine Rubikaite from East Sussex, Dean Storey from Herefordshire, Millie Preese from Shropshire and Tracey Longhurst of Surrey.
