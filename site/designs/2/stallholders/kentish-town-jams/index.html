Cheese accompaniments

Kentish Town Jams

Stef’s Kitchen, based in Kentish Town, creates a delightful array of homemade jams and preserves, designed to complement cheese boards or add a burst of flavour to everyday meals. Using seasonal fruits and traditional techniques, Stef’s products offer an authentic, artisanal touch. Their jams are known for their balance of sweetness and acidity, making them a perfect pairing for cheeses and charcuterie.
