Cheese producers and mongers

Cheese on the Wey

Small artisan cheesemaker from Farnham using pasteurised milk from Pierrepoint Farm. The range includes Tilston Blue; Millie, a cheese similar to Caerphilly; Weywood, a soft cheese with a cider washed rind and Tommie which is a seasonal Alpine style cheese.
