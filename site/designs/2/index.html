The UK’s only specialist cheese market

The market is held every third Sunday of the month between 9.30 am and 3.00 pm at The Old Market Place, Chiswick High Road (opposite M&S), W4 2DR.
A unique destination for cheese lovers the market celebrates the finest artisan cheeses from across the UK and beyond. Come and explore a delicious array of flavours in the charming surroundings of Chiswick.
