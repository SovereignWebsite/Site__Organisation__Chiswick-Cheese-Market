Cheeses + Tasting Notes / 16 November 2026

Tasting Notes: November

We had a wonderful tasting on Cheesewick HQ this month with Brindisa. We wanted to introduce market guests to unusual sheep’s milk cheeses from Spain, proving that there’s much more to Spanish cheese than Manchego.

In case you forgot and want to order some next time, here’s what we were tasting:

Zamorano Vicente Pastor, PDO
A traditional PDO Zamorano made from raw milk produced by the family’s own flock of indigenous Castellana sheep, grazing the plains of Zamora in northwestern Spain. Unlike many commercially finished cheeses, it retains its naturally moulded, unpainted rind. After approximately seven months of maturation, it develops a firm yet buttery texture, with a distinctive slight acidity and lingering cellar-aged notes that reflect its traditional production. Winner: Silver at the World Cheese Awards 2023

Torta de Barros
Made from raw sheep’s milk sourced from a single local farm in Extremadura, Torta de Barros uses cardoon flower (Cynara cardunculus) as its natural coagulant. This creates its characteristic almost-liquid, spoonable centre and a delicate, lingering bitterness that balances the richness of the sheep’s milk. Its distinctive character earned it the title of Best Spanish Cheese at the 2018 International Cheese Awards.Winner: Silver at the World Cheese Awards 2023.

“Pi” Mould-Ripened Raw Sheep’s Milk Cheese
Pi challenges conventional expectations of Manchega sheep’s milk cheeses. Produced at Finca Hualdo using milk from its own flock, it develops a mould-ripened rind and an unusually silky, semi-soft interior. Its flavour evolves from fresh, buttery and citrus notes towards earthy, herbal and nutty complexity near the rind. Awarded three stars at Great Taste in both 2025 and 2026, it demonstrates the remarkable versatility of raw Manchega sheep’s milk

When they sold out of the Pi, we added:

Villarejo Rosemary Cheese 

A Manchego-style cheese coated in Iberico pork fat and rosemary. The dairy is in Villarejo de Fuentes, in the heart of La Mancha and also produces authentic artisan Manchego cheese. They have 1,500 square meters of land over which the sheep graze freely ensuring superb milk. The cheese has a dense and lightly crunchy texture with buttery and herbal aromas.
