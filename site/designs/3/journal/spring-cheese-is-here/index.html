News / 16 April 2025

Spring Cheese is Here

Spring milk means sweet, rich flavours – perfect for cheeses like Wild Garlic Yarg, Yoredale Wensleydale, Rachel, and Golden Cross. Don’t miss Harley’s (@oxfordcheeseco) Easter offers: 2 chilli jams for £5 and St Charles cheese portions for just £5. Vegan? Try @sophies.delight’s wild garlic cheese and enter their Easter competition. And yes, @sexypicklesuk’s crunchy dill gherkins are back – but in short supply!
