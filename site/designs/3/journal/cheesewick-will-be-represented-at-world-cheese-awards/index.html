Spotlight + Awards + News / 12 November 2024

‘Cheesewick’ will be represented at World Cheese Awards

Our team’s collective love of food has turned into a passion for cheese and cheese knowledge.

Lucy Cufflin was among the judges at the 2024 World Cheese Awards in Portugal and is a Level 2 Member of the Academy of Cheese. Our Market Curator and Manager, Jules Kane judged the 2022 Bath & West British Cheese Awards and Abi Pitcher is another Academy of Cheese Level 2 Member.

We have truly become champions for cheese.

“I am thrilled to have been selected as a judge at the auspicious World Cheese Awards in Portugal later this week. This is a great honour both for me and for Chiswick Cheese Market. I will be judging alongside some of the world’s truly ‘big cheeses’ so I am doing some extra homework, brushing up on all my cheese knowledge and I don’t mind admitting I am a bit nervous but mostly just very excited – Watch this space for an update!”

– Lucy Cufflin
