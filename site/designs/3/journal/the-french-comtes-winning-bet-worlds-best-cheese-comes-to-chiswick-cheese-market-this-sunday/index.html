The French Comte’s winning bet: World’s Best Cheese comes to Chiswick Cheese Market this Sunday

f you haven’t already heard, a 24 month Comté PDO Fort des Rousses from Fromageries Arnaud was crowned the winner of the 2026 Guild of Fine Food’sorld Cheese Awards having beat out 5,330 entrants in Córdoba, Spain.

In a stunning boon for the Chiswick Cheese Market and for our customers, one of our traders actually stock this cheese and you can buy it tomorrow!

The French Comte, who specialise in French cheeses, are lucky enough to be stockists of this globally acclaimed cheese. However, this happy event doesn’t come about as haphazardly as you might imagine.

Started to showcase the produce from their native Franche-Comté region of eastern France, Roberto and Joel return each June or July to tour cheese caves in Jura. They taste cheese and choose the ones they would like to sell but the trick is, that was nearly two years ago. Like wine futures, they have to believe in the great blossoming of this cheese to come.

They visit many different producers and they have worked with Fromageries Arnaud in the past. They discussed the weather, the pasture, the herd and his hopes for the cheese with Jean-Charles Arnaud, a fourth generation cheesemaker, and they once they made their choice, the cheese was marked as theirs – for them and them alone – and because of that, FOR US!

Please come and grab your slice of world-wide winning Alpine cheese heaven – chosen when the hills were alive with flowers and music.

Roberto of The French Comte – he can pick a winner from a year off!
