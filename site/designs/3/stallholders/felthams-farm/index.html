Cheese producers and mongers

Feltham’s Farm

Feltham’s Farm are organic cheesemakers from the Blackmore Vale in South Somerset, best known for Renegade Monk, their multi award-winning ale-washed cheese made with local organic cow’s milk. Their range also includes Rebel Nun, Gert Lush, La Fresca Margarita and Lucky Marcel – all Great Taste Award winners, with Lucky Marcel taking Super Gold at the World Cheese Awards 2024.

Sustainability sits at the heart of the farm, powered by solar energy, ground source heat pumps and rare breed pigs that recycle whey, alongside tree planting and organic farming practices certified by the Soil Association. Founders of the Organic Cheesemakers’ Network, Feltham’s Farm champion organic principles in British cheese.

They regularly trade at London farmers’ markets and are delighted to share their cheeses with new customers – including their light, fresh La Fresca Margarita, a standout organic queso fresco.
