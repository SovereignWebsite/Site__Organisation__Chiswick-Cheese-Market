The Cornish Gouda Co

Founded by a Dutch family, based in Looe in Cornwall. Giel Spierings crafts traditional Dutch Gouda with a Cornish twist. Using milk from their own cows, they focus on creating high-quality cheeses with diverse flavour profiles from maturation or adding herbs and spices.
